Make your video library smaller, safely

shrink-your-shit converts videos to a more efficient format so they take less space. It checks every new file before it replaces anything, and it keeps your original for 7 days so you can get it back.

What to expectMost libraries shrink by about 10-20%. Files that came from Blu-ray rips can shrink more. We tell you the real number once files are done.
